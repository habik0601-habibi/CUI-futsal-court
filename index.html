Booking rules

  • Maximum 1 booking per day and 2 per week (Mon–Fri) per student.
  • Book up to 7 days ahead. Past slots cannot be booked.
  • Several students can request the same slot. Your department head and the Sports Centre decide whose request is approved; only a confirmed booking locks the slot, and the other requests for it are declined.
  • A booking that is not confirmed by the time the slot starts expires automatically.
  • Missing a confirmed slot (no-show) bans you from booking for 14 days.

How approval works

1. Student requests a slot → 2. Department sports head approves → 3. Sports Centre head gives final approval → Confirmed.
